Publication Workflow

1 Author submits a research paper
2 Editor performs initial screening
3 Reviewers evaluate the assigned paper version
4 Author revises when changes are requested
5 Editor makes the official final decision
6 Accepted paper completes copyediting
7 Journal Owner manages production and publication

Role-Based Workspace

Built for every stage of the publication process

Each business role receives access to the tools and responsibilities required for its part of the workflow.

01

Author

Register independently, submit research papers, view anonymous Reviewer feedback, upload revised versions, complete copyediting corrections, and track publication progress.

02

Reviewer

Accept or decline review assignments, review assigned paper versions, provide comments, and recommend Accept, Reject, or Revision.

03

Editor

Perform initial screening, assign Reviewers, monitor review rounds, make official final decisions, and manage copyediting.

04

Journal Owner

Manage Journals and Conferences, create and manage Editor and Reviewer accounts, assign Editors, monitor submissions, manage production, and publish completed papers.

Main Features

Complete research publication workflow

RPMS manages the complete research paper lifecycle while keeping editorial responsibilities and access clearly separated between users.

01

Paper Submission

Authors select an available Journal or Conference, enter their research information, and securely upload the manuscript as a PDF file.

02

Reviewer Assignment

Editors can assign multiple Reviewers to a review round and monitor each Reviewer's assignment and completion status.

03

Review Rounds & Versions

Every revision creates a new paper version and review round, allowing the complete review history to remain available throughout the process.

04

Revision & Feedback

Reviewers can request revisions with comments. Authors can review the feedback and submit a revised manuscript for another review round.

05

Editorial Decisions

Reviewer recommendations support the review process, while the Editor records the official Accepted or Rejected final decision.

06

Copyediting

Editors manage copyediting for accepted papers, request corrections when required, and Authors submit corrected manuscript versions.

07

Production & Publishing

After copyediting is completed, Journal Owners manage production and publish the final paper with its publication record.

08

Journal & Editorial Management

Journal Owners can manage multiple Journals and Conferences, create Editor and Reviewer accounts, and assign one or multiple Editors to publications.

09

Secure & Isolated Access

Paper files are protected by role-based access, while each Journal Owner's Editor and Reviewer accounts remain isolated from other owners.

Account Management

Clear and controlled user access

RPMS separates public Author registration from organization-managed editorial accounts.

01

Author Registration

Authors can register through the public registration page and submit papers to available publications.

02

Editorial Accounts

Journal Owners create and manage their own Editor and Reviewer accounts for use within their publication environment.

03

Owner Isolation

Editors and Reviewers belonging to one Journal Owner cannot be assigned to publications owned by another Journal Owner.
