Paper Workflow

From Submission to Publication

Your paper can move through multiple review and revision rounds before the Editor makes the official final decision.

1. Submit Paper → 2. Initial Screening → 3. Peer Review → 4. Revision if Required → 5. Final Decision → 6. Copyediting → 7. Production & Publication

Reviewer recommendations do not directly accept or reject your paper. The Editor makes the official final decision after the review process is completed.
