Review Process

  1. Open the assigned review.
  2. Accept or decline the assignment.
  3. Check the review round and assigned paper version.
  4. Read the protected PDF file.
  5. Write your review comments.
  6. Submit your recommendation.
Review Recommendations

Available Review Outcomes

Recommend Accept

Recommend acceptance when the paper has satisfactorily addressed the required academic and technical concerns.

Request Revision

Request changes and provide comments for the Author. A revised paper can be submitted as a new version and returned for another review round.

Recommend Reject

Recommend rejection when the paper has major issues that should prevent it from progressing further in the review process.

Review Rounds

Revised Papers May Return to You

When an Author submits a revised paper, RPMS creates a new paper version and a new review round. You may be assigned again to review the revised version, so always check the displayed review round and paper version before completing your review.

Reviewer Workflow

From Assignment to Recommendation

Assigned → Accept Assignment → Review Paper Version → Add Comments → Submit Recommendation → Review Completed

Your recommendation does not directly accept or reject the paper. After the review process is completed, the Editor makes the official final decision.
